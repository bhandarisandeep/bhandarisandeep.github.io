How to post this, and compatibility notes
  • AirDrop or Files-app the downloaded file to your phone, then upload it from the Instagram app. Don’t send it through WhatsApp first — it recompresses images and strips the glow.
  • Only HDR screens show the glow: Chrome on an HDR Mac, and HDR phones (iPhone, recent Android). Non-HDR screens just show the normal photo — that’s by design.
  • On a Mac, the Preview app needs macOS 15+ to show the glow. Chrome always works, on any macOS version.
  • The optional EXR is only used if it actually contains highlight data brighter than your photo and matches its pixel size — otherwise the glow is synthesized from the photo itself.
